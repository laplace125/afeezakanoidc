Artifact 1 — Analysis

Needs Analysis

The learning problem, target learners, learner needs, context, and performance gap that shaped this capstone.

Identified learning need

Secondary school teachers are increasingly using generative AI tools such as ChatGPT and Gemini to support lesson preparation. However, some teachers have trouble writing effective prompts and evaluating AI-generated instructional materials.

In some cases, teachers may accept AI-generated content without checking its accuracy, curriculum alignment, appropriateness for their learners, or suitability for the intended lesson objectives.

Therefore, there is a need for a short, practical learning experience that helps teachers use generative AI more effectively and critically when preparing lessons.

Target learners

The target learners are secondary school teachers with varying levels of digital and artificial intelligence experience.

Most learners are expected to have experience with traditional lesson planning but may have limited experience using generative AI for instructional purposes.

The learners are adult professionals. Adults value practical examples, demonstrations, and activities that can be immediately applied to their teaching.

Learner needs

The learners need to:

  • Understand the basic purpose and limitations of generative AI.
  • Create clear and purposeful AI prompts.
  • Use AI to support different lesson-planning tasks.
  • Evaluate AI-generated instructional content.
  • Adapt AI-generated content to suit their learners and curriculum.

Learning context

The learning experience will be designed as a short digital course that can be accessed using a smartphone or computer.

The course may be completed independently or used as part of a short teacher professional-development session.

The learning experience will use demonstrations, examples, guided practice, and a practical assessment rather than relying mainly on theoretical explanations.

Performance gap

Current performance: Some teachers use AI for lesson preparation without consistently providing sufficient instructions or critically evaluating the generated content.

Desired performance: Teachers use AI purposefully by creating effective prompts, evaluating the resulting content, and adapting it to their learners' needs.

The instructional intervention focuses on developing practical AI prompting and evaluation skills.

Preliminary learning goal

By the end of the learning experience, teachers will be able to:

  1. Use generative AI to support lesson planning by creating effective prompts.
  2. Evaluate AI-generated instructional content.
  3. Adapt the content to meet the needs of their learners.
