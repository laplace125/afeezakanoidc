My instructional design capstone

This project represents my application of instructional design principles to a real learning need. I began by considering the learners, their needs, the learning problem, and the desired outcomes — then used an instructional design process to structure the learning experience, develop materials, and build assessments aligned with clear objectives.

Capstone Project

Learning Experience Design

The learning need

This project addresses a learning need by providing learners with a structured, engaging experience rather than simply presenting information — helping them understand the subject matter, practise what they've learned, receive feedback, and demonstrate achievement of the intended outcomes.

Target learners

Designed for learners who need a clear, practical, and accessible approach to the subject, taking into account differences in prior knowledge, learning pace, digital familiarity, and learning ability.

Learning goal

To enable learners to develop meaningful understanding and practical competence in the selected subject area.

Instructional approach

A learner-centred approach combining clear explanations, examples, guided practice, learner activities, assessment, feedback, and reflection — supported where appropriate by technology and AI-assisted tools.

Learning objectives

At the end of the learning experience, learners should be able to:

  • Explain the key concepts introduced in the learning experience.
  • Apply the concepts to appropriate situations or problems.
  • Demonstrate their understanding through practical activities.
  • Complete assessments aligned with the learning objectives.
  • Reflect on their learning and identify areas for improvement.

From learning need to learning experience

I approached the project as an instructional design problem rather than simply a content-development exercise — identifying the learning need, defining desired outcomes, planning the experience, developing the materials, and considering how effectiveness could be evaluated.

  1. 01

    Analysis

    I considered who the learners are, what they already know, what they need to learn, what learning problem needs to be addressed, what constraints may affect the experience, and what technology and resources are available. This stage established the foundation for the project.

  2. 02

    Design

    I defined the learning goal, developed measurable learning objectives, structured the learning content, selected appropriate instructional strategies, planned learning activities, and designed assessments aligned with the objectives.

  3. 03

    Development

    I created and organised the learning materials required for the experience — instructional content, activities, examples, assessment items, and supporting digital resources — considering how technology and AI could support development while keeping human review and instructional judgement central.

  4. 04

    Implementation

    The learning experience was designed to be practical and accessible to learners, with attention to how learners interact with the content, activities, assessments, and feedback.

  5. 05

    Evaluation

    Evaluation focuses on whether the learning experience achieves its intended purpose, using evidence such as learner performance, assessment results, learner feedback, achievement of objectives, areas of difficulty, and opportunities for improvement.

Technology & AI

Technology played an important role in the development of this project. I explored digital tools to support content development, presentation, assessment, collaboration, and learner engagement. Artificial intelligence was also considered as a supporting tool in the instructional design process.

However, AI was treated as a support tool rather than a replacement for instructional judgement. Human review remains important for accuracy, relevance, appropriateness, learner context, and alignment with learning objectives.

AI supported tasks such as

  • Brainstorming
  • Content organisation
  • Generating examples
  • Developing assessment ideas
  • Creating alternative explanations
  • Reviewing instructional content
  • Supporting accessibility

What I believe about learning

Effective learning should go beyond simply presenting information. Learners need opportunities to understand, practise, apply, receive feedback, and reflect.

Learner-centred

Designed around the needs, abilities, and context of the learner.

Practical

Connected to situations where learners can apply what they have learned.

Engaging

Uses appropriate activities, examples, technology, and interaction to maintain learner involvement.

Accessible

Presents information clearly and considers different learning needs.

Measurable

Uses clear learning objectives and assessments to determine whether learning has occurred.

My capstone reflection

Working on this capstone helped me understand that instructional design is more than creating attractive learning materials. A good learning experience begins with understanding the learner and the learning problem.

One of the important lessons I gained was the importance of alignment. The learning objectives, instructional activities, learning materials, and assessments should work together toward the same intended outcomes.

The project also strengthened my understanding of how technology and AI can support instructional design. At the same time, I learned that technology should serve the learning objective rather than become the objective itself.

My background as a classroom educator helped me think about practical learner needs, while the instructional design process helped me approach those needs in a more systematic way.

What I would improve

If I were to develop the project again, I would collect more learner feedback during the development and testing stages.

I would also conduct additional usability testing and use the results to refine the learning experience.

This project has strengthened my interest in designing effective digital learning experiences and has given me a stronger foundation for future instructional design projects.

Where I am going

I intend to continue developing my skills in instructional design, educational technology, AI-supported learning, and digital course development. My goal is to combine my experience as an educator with instructional design principles to create learning experiences that help learners understand difficult concepts, practise effectively, and develop practical skills.
